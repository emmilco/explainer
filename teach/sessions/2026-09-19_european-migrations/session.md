## Narrative

What ran:

1. Arc scaffold — two demic replacement waves (Anatolian farmers; Yamnaya steppe) fixed the
   three-way ancestry floor by 2500 BC; Rome as mobility-without-replacement; Migration Period
   split three ways; the 800s as hinge.
2. Migration Period verdicts — Visigoths, Vandals, Ostrogoths, Lombards were elite layers that
   assimilated; Anglo-Saxon England and the Slavic Balkans were demic, because families moved
   at scale into collapsed demography. Rule offered: "armies leave surnames; wagon trains
   leave genes."
3. Viking settlement modes — raiding (no trace) / elite grant (Normandy, Rus') / farm
   settlement (Danelaw, Ireland, Iceland, Orkney).
4. Cast of pre-Charlemagne peoples (table); Celts in depth (language vs. culture vs. genes;
   Hallstatt/La Tène; expansion 4th–3rd c. BC; Roman conquest; insular survival); Germanic
   peoples and the Goths to 376/378.
